# Code of Conduct

**行为准则**

## Our Pledge（我们的承诺）

OpenField exists to preserve what people say, with dignity. We extend that same dignity to everyone who builds, debates, and reviews here — and absolute respect to the participants whose voices this project carries.
OpenField 的使命是有尊严地保存人们说出的话。我们把同样的尊严给予每一位在此建设、辩论与评审的人，并绝对尊重项目所承载声音的每一位受访者。

## Expected Behavior（我们期待）

- Debate principles, not people. Cite the clause; argue the point.
  对原则辩论，不对人攻击。引用条款，针对论点。
- Be rigorous and honest — especially about AI output: never present AI-generated material as human-confirmed research.
  严谨诚实——尤其对 AI 输出：绝不把 AI 生成材料冒充为经人工确认的研究成果。
- Treat field material with respect in every discussion; never mock the people behind the data.
  在一切讨论中尊重田野材料；绝不嘲弄数据背后的人。
- Never share participant-identifying material outside the vault's protections.
  绝不在资料库保护机制之外传播可识别受访者的材料。
- Communicate in good faith, in either or both languages.
  中英文皆可，善意沟通。

## Unacceptable Behavior（不可接受）

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind.
  任何形式的骚扰、歧视或人身攻击。
- Publishing or linking real participant data, or attempting to deanonymize it.
  发布或链接真实受访者数据，或试图对其去匿名化。
- Deliberately violating the Six Non-Negotiables (PRINCIPLES.md §2) or masking such violations.
  故意违反六大不可妥协项（PRINCIPLES.md 第 2 节）或为其遮掩。
- Misrepresenting an affiliation with the project or its research outputs.
  虚构与本项目或其研究成果的关系。

## Scope（适用范围）

All project spaces: issues, PRs, reviews, and any channel representing OpenField.
本仓库的全部空间：议题、PR、评审，以及一切代表 OpenField 的场合。

## Enforcement（执行）

Report violations privately to the maintainer [@allengaller](https://github.com/allengaller) — not in public issues. The maintainer reviews, decides, and records the outcome; sanctions range from a warning to a permanent ban. Decisions may be appealed once, in writing, citing what has changed.
请私下向维护者 [@allengaller](https://github.com/allengaller) 报告，勿在公开议题中发帖。维护者负责评审、裁决并记录结果；处理从警告到永久封禁。可就裁决书面申诉一次，需说明新的变化。

## Attribution（渊源）

Structure follows the Contributor Covenant, rewritten in this project's own voice.
体例承自 Contributor Covenant，措辞依本项目的语气重写。
